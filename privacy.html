DIPRR by Cheeyell — Digital Information & Account Passwords Record Register

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Who we are: DIPRR by Cheeyell ("DIPRR", "we") is provided by Ekaayi Healthcare Private Limited, India.

Contact and Grievance Officer: pa.ekaayi@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.

1. The short version

  • Your projects, files and records are stored in your own Google account (Google Drive and Google Sheets), not on our servers.
  • Passwords and identity documents in DIPRR vaults are encrypted on your device before they are saved. We cannot read them, and neither can Google.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, track you, or use your content to train AI.
  • The DIPRR iPhone app has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

3. Legal basis (India — DPDP Act 2023 and DPDP Rules 2025)

We process personal data with your consent, given when you set up and use DIPRR, and for the legitimate uses the Act allows. You can withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it, by stopping use and deleting your data (see section 6).

Where the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 still apply, passwords are stored only end-to-end encrypted and are never accessible to us.

4. Other people's data

DIPRR lets you record accounts belonging to family members. You confirm that you have their knowledge and permission. Emergency access works only between people you have both added to your DIPRR.

5. Sharing

  • Share links you create show the selected project to anyone with the link until it expires or you stop it.
  • Emergency contacts you name can get read-only access to your private vault after the waiting period you chose, unless you refuse.
  • Service providers: Google (hosting in your own account) and Apple (App Store and TestFlight distribution). Nobody else.
  • We will disclose data only if required by Indian law, and only what we can access. We cannot access encrypted vault contents.

6. Your rights

You can access, correct, export (Export, Backup) and delete (Recently deleted, then Drive trash) your data at any time. On iPhone, Settings → "Remove offline pack" deletes the offline copy, and deleting the app removes everything it stored. You can nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to (Emergency access, or tell us in writing).

For anything else, contact the Grievance Officer at pa.ekaayi@gmail.com. If you're not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

7. Security

Encryption: AES-256-GCM with PBKDF2-SHA256 key derivation, and RSA-OAEP for emergency access. Passwordless email codes with rate limits, an activity log, and sign-out on all devices. The iPhone app locks the offline vault whenever you leave the app and clears copied passwords after 30 seconds.

No system is perfectly secure. Protect your Google account with 2-Step Verification and keep your recovery codes safe. If you lose both your passphrase and your recovery code, nobody can recover your vault.

8. If something goes wrong

If we become aware of a personal-data breach affecting you, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires, describing what happened, the likely impact, and what we and you can do.

9. Where data is stored

Your data is stored by Google in your own Google account and may be processed on Google's servers outside India. We rely on Google's safeguards and comply with any transfer restrictions notified under Indian law.

10. Retention

Your data stays until you delete it. Deleted items are kept for 30 days in Recently deleted, then removed. Google Drive keeps trashed files for 30 days.

11. Children

DIPRR is only for people aged 18 or over. Don't create an account or use DIPRR if you are under 18.

12. Changes

We'll post changes here and update the date. For significant changes, we'll notify you by email.
